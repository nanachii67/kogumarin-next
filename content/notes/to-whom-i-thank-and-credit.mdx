When I was starting out, I was so confused with how I wanted it to look like, I guess because I had a lot of references to work with — namely, with Apple’s design language. It always has been so minimal, clean, and is an eye candy. But designing a site comes with a cost; being too obsessive with how things should be placed. Some pages would taken me to design for a day, even taking me a week to do or complete! Being hyper-fixated to one thing is a blessing and a curse… As how people would see it, “Perfectionism”.

It would only be natural that creating this site doesn’t came all from me, but also from many people — friends, and families. I thank them with all from the bottom of my heart.
