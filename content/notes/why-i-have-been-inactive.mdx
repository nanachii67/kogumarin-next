So you might be wondering why I have not made a lot of posts or shared any, and why I have not made any music for a long while.

To begin with, there have been a lot of things happening in the background that are more than music or being in the persona of Koguma Rin. First, I am constantly in development for my thesis, making me shift my paradigm from music to programming and vice versa.

Not only about my paradigm about what I do, but also on how I do certain stuff, as well as how excited I am about producing my first album, it almost felt like I am pushing myself too hard, leading to frequent burnouts.

I am very sorry that I have been inactive for a while. I'll try to come back soon and keep in touch with everyone. Furthermore, now that I have had the chance to switch systems from Windows to Mac, I want to experiment with new styles of music.

Anyways, that's it from me for now. I might be more active when Christmas comes. Come join my Discord sometime so we can keep in touch!
