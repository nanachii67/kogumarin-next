When I told you guys that I would not be releasing my stash on major music platforms, it is due to personal financial constraints on distribution and me not having any return of profit from it.

Now, the times are changing.

After commercial breakthroughs with [Jane Cerys/Kotomi](https://linktr.ee/janecerys), I am announcing to be releasing my music on major streaming platforms, (e.g., Spotify, and Apple Music) where original music will go under KOGS. That said, I was also invited by [project//aspyr](https://linktr.ee/projectaspyr) to push music that focuses on remixes, with that label.

Throughout the weeks and months that will follow, the discography of Koguma Rin will be distributed and can be streamed on platforms you listen to.
