Hello to the listeners of Koguma Rin, I know the anticipation and the expectations that you all have been building for my very first album titled, "Rin's Silly Tunes." I myself have been excited about writing and producing the additional tracks other than the EPs, however, with the sign of it being already delayed back in July following unforseen events, this project is better put to rest.

The hardware where I've been writing the album finally made its last run last week. Since the SSDs where my project files, audio samples, and plugins are stored are internal, this will be harder for me to retrieve.

I sincerly apologise for the expectation you might have had before this event, and also a lesson to create file backups now and then. Looking forward, I also plan on doing something new on the side, as a new beginning on pushing bounds in music production. I'Il see you sometime soon.
