When I told you guys that I would not be releasing my stash on major music platforms, it is due to personal financial constraints on distribution and me not having any return of profit from it.

That said, I was also invited by project//aspyr to push music that focuses on remixes, with that label.

Throughout the weeks and months that will follow, the discography of Koguma Rin will be distributed and can be streamed on platforms you listen to.
